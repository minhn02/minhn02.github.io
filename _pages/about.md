I am a first year PhD student in the ASE department at the University of Texas at Austin. 

I'm broadly interested in multi-agent robotics, particularly how to enable their efficient interactions through game theory and machine learning. Some recent projects include [enabling quadrupeds to cooperatively tow a box with multi-agent reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18221) and [using diffusion models to design computer chips](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12282).

On the side, I enjoy bouldering and upgrading my home lab.
